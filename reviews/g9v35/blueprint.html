D3.1 · Recall / classify · Multi-step reasoning

Her move: Pick or reconstruct the right fact, category, definition, unit, sign or condition, deciding by its defining condition.

template ref
QRT-RETRIEVE-D3
attempt pattern
Main cycle: classify at least 4 fresh cases drawn from at least 2 subtopics, including one near-neighbour pair and one exception; verdict + deciding condition for each; at least one case reaches its condition in two dependent steps.
protected
Each verdict and its deciding condition.
support may show
The candidate categories and the definitions' wording; never which case goes where.
band recipe
concept 1-2 + representation 0-1 + chain 1-2 + algebra 0 + trap 2 = 6-7
note
Real exam Recall questions often score 4-5 (D2); the D3 band is usually carried by the Core1B attempt, not by the exam question.

D4.1 · Recall / classify · High-transfer / exception-sensitive

Her move: Pick or reconstruct the right fact, category, definition, unit, sign or condition, deciding by its defining condition.

template ref
QRT-RETRIEVE-D4
attempt pattern
Classification whose deciding condition must be read from an unfamiliar context or representation, where an exception overturns the familiar verdict.
protected
The classification and the exception that decides it.
support may show
Constraints and invariants only.
band recipe
concept 2 + representation 2 + chain 2 + algebra 0 + trap 2 = 8
note
Rare by construction: 8 is reachable only with every non-algebra component at 2. If the translation or the model choice becomes the decisive act, reclassify (D4.5 or D4.4). An empty D4.1 is reported as empty, never inflated.
