The physics is sound in all seven packs. I re-worked every exam answer (58 items) and every worked example, model answer, close-the-idea task and boundary test. All agree with the builders. Signs, units and conditions are right. The two known input errors are handled correctly in every pack:

  • Book §5 says friction never does positive work. Every pack that touches this teaches the correct rule instead: friction can do positive work on one body, and the friction pair together never does. Places: D3.1 S1-B4, MAIN E and F; D3.2 E2; D3.4 Mo1.
  • The spring's own work while it is stretched or squeezed from natural length is −½kx². This is stated with the right sign in D3.3 A3 (before-you-start and ledger), D3.6 spring-B1 (½kx₁² − ½kx₂²) and D3.6 M78. Where a pack writes +½kx², it is the work done ON the spring by the hand (D3.7 J3), which is correct.

The one systemic failure is protection. In six of the seven packs, the predict's "defensible answer" gives away the verdict, the value or the protected step of the attempt that comes straight after it. render_tile.py (lines 266–268) puts the defensible answer in an open <details> box under "1 · Predict". That box sits above the attempt and is not locked until she saves her first answer. So she reads her own protected step before she tries it.

Only D3.1 avoids this: its builder set each predict outside the attempt's cases, and raised the missing rule as D3.1 GAP-3.

There are two ways to fix it. (a) Rewrite each predict so that it uses a case outside the attempt. (b) Change the renderer to lock the predict's defensible answer until the attempt is saved. Fix (b) is one change that clears every predict blocker below. Fix (a) is still better teaching. I recommend both, plus the GAP-3 rule in common.reveal_boundaries.

A second, lesser pattern, rated MAJOR: in D3.3, D3.4, D3.6 and D3.7, Core1A teaches the START question's deciding step almost word for word. When she meets the START question again at the end, it tests recall rather than transfer. The blueprint has no rule against this yet (D3.1 GAP-3, part 1). The Oracle should decide on it.
